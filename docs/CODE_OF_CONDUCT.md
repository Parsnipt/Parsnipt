# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Parsnipt community will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Respecting privacy and personal boundaries

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassing, insulting, or demeaning language and conduct
- Any form of discrimination based on personal characteristics
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent (doxxing)
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review and investigate all complaints and will respond appropriately. All project team members are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at [email - to be added]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
